02 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

A great film starts
with the right crew.

Your next job shouldn’t depend on who you already know.

The industry runs on people. Working with familiar faces and trusted collaborators is one of the best parts of the job. But even the people you’ve worked with for years won’t always have another project lined up—or a role for you when they do.

Expanding your network opens the door to new collaborators and opportunities. But finding those people still means relying on word of mouth and lists, searching through Instagram stories and Facebook groups, and reaching out to people from previous sets.

If you’re moving between cities, building a local network can mean starting from scratch. FilmMeet is being built to make it easier to meet crew in your new city, connect with local productions, and find your next job on set.

Much of the independent film industry runs on freelance work. When one production wraps, the search for the next job on set begins.

FilmMeet brings professional profiles, crew opportunities, and industry connections into one place—helping you expand your network beyond your last set and find the people behind your next one.

For independent productions, that connection works both ways. FilmMeet will help teams find local indie crew with the skills and experience their project needs—whether they’re filming in their home city or working somewhere new.

Contest rules & eligibility

FilmMeet Short Film Contest · Updated November 5, 2026

01Who can enter

Entrants must be 18 or older. Filmmakers from anywhere in the world are welcome to submit. Students, first-time filmmakers and experienced filmmakers are welcome. The person submitting must be authorized to enter the film on behalf of its creators. FilmMeet organizers and judges may not enter.

02Film requirements

Films must be 30 minutes or less, including credits, and must have been completed within the five years preceding the submission deadline. All genres are welcome, including narrative, documentary, animation and experimental shorts. If the film’s primary language is not English, English subtitles must be provided with the submitted film.

Previously released films are eligible, including films that have screened at festivals, appeared online, or been publicly distributed. No premiere status is required. Limit one film entry per person.

03AI film policy

AI-generated films are not eligible for this year’s FilmMeet Short Film Contest. At this time, we believe live-action and AI-generated films are difficult to evaluate fairly within the same competition. We may consider accepting AI-generated films or introducing a separate category in future contests.

04How to submit

Submissions open December 1, 2026, on the FilmMeet website at filmmeetapp.com. Entry is free. Use the contest submission form below and provide the film title, runtime, synopsis, director name, contact email and a working screener link. Include the password if required and keep the film accessible through March 1, 2027, or any revised winner-announcement date communicated by FilmMeet.

Submit the completed film by February 1, 2027, at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time. Incomplete, late or inaccessible entries may be ineligible.

05Rights and permissions

You retain ownership of your film. By entering, you confirm you have the rights and permissions needed for the film, including music, footage, artwork and participant releases. FilmMeet may share your screener privately with contest staff and judges for evaluation. Any public screening or use of clips, stills or other promotional materials requires a separate agreement with you.

06Judging and awards

Judging criteria are storytelling, originality, creative vision and overall execution, weighted equally. Production budget does not determine eligibility. Judges must disclose conflicts and step aside from affected entries. Ties will be resolved by the higher storytelling score, then panel review.

The Best Short Film winner receives $1,000 USD, early access to the FilmMeet app, an award certificate, and an official FilmMeet contest laurel. The second-place winner receives exclusive use of the FilmMeet app, an award certificate, and an official FilmMeet contest laurel. The third-place winner receives an award certificate and an official FilmMeet contest laurel.

07Results and entry conduct

Winners are scheduled to be announced March 1, 2027. The announcement may be delayed if judges need additional time. Selected filmmakers will be contacted using the email provided. Any revised announcement date will be posted on this website and communicated to entrants.

An entry may be disqualified for false information, infringement or failure to meet these rules. Any material schedule or rule changes will be communicated to entrants, along with any withdrawal options that apply.

08Contact and agreement

Questions: milan@filmmeetapp.com. Entrant information will be used to administer the contest, review submissions and contact filmmakers about their entries. By submitting, you confirm that you have read and accept these contest rules.
